## Posting tips

- Post in 2–3 groups max per day; comment on others' posts before
  promoting (group norms matter — don't be the first poster to sell).
- Reply to every comment with a DM: "Want me to check your site? Takes 30
  seconds to add your URL — [link]."
- Collect the sample audit (docs/sample-audit.html) → print to PDF → attach
  as the proof post in groups. Anonymized data only.
- Track which group/post drove signups: add `?src=fb-group-name` to the
  link (or use the source field) and note it in leads.csv.
